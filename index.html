IELTS Advantage

Графики: line, bar, pie, table

Структура — Charts Lesson и Task 1 in 5 Easy Steps. Фразы для заполнения собраны мной из их образцов и Grammar Guide. В каналах Эрдли и VBoostLab текстового разбора графиков нет.

Сначала один вопрос: есть ли даты?

Dynamic — данные за несколько моментов времени: описываем тренды, времена по датам (прошлое — Past Simple, прогноз — will / is projected to). Static — один момент: сравниваем категории, трендов нет. Если дат нет совсем — Present Simple.

1. Introduction — одно предложение, перефраз задания

The [line graph / bar chart / pie charts / table] [shows / illustrates / compares] [what is measured] in [place] [between YEAR and YEAR / in YEAR].

The line graph shows energy consumption by fuel type in the United States from 1980-2008, with projected use until 2030.

2. Overview — 2–3 предложения, без цифр

Самый важный абзац. Выберите 2–3 самые очевидные вещи: общий рост или падение, самое большое и самое маленькое, главное различие, заметное исключение. Даты можно, цифры нельзя.

С датами
Overall, [A] [rose / fell] over the period, while [B] [did the opposite / remained relatively stable]. [C] was the [highest / most popular] throughout.
Без дат
Overall, [A] was the [largest / most common] [category], whereas [B] was the [smallest]. It is also clear that [general comparison].

Overall, there was a general decrease in the percentage of people who donated money over the two time periods. However, the pattern differs between the three age groups before 50 and the two age groups after the age of 50.

3. Body 1 — детали первой главной черты, с цифрами

In [first year], [A] stood at [number]. It then [rose sharply] to [number] in [year], before [falling] to [number] by [last year].
Без дат
[A] had the highest [figure] at [number], followed by [B] with [number]. [C] and [D] were considerably lower, at [number] and [number] respectively.

4. Body 2 — детали второй главной черты, сравнение или исключение

By contrast, [B] started at [number] and [declined steadily], reaching [number] in [year]. [C], meanwhile, remained relatively stable at around [number].

The exception to this general trend downwards was among the 51-65-year-olds and over 65s.

Итого 4 абзаца и 8–12 предложений. Проверка: незнакомый человек по вашему тексту должен суметь нарисовать график.

Тренды: две конструкции

There was a [gradual / sharp / slight / steady / dramatic / significant] [rise / increase / growth / fall / drop / decline / fluctuation] in [noun phrase].
[Noun phrase] [rose / increased / grew / climbed / fell / dropped / declined / plummeted] [gradually / sharply / slightly / steadily / dramatically / significantly].

Не пишите to conclude и не делайте выводов — это только для эссе.

IELTS Advantage

Два графика сразу

Источник: IELTS Task 1 Multiple Graphs.

Правило

Из каждого графика берём по две самые заметные черты и пишем только о них. Один график — один абзац; данные разных графиков в одном абзаце не смешиваем. Сравнение между графиками — только если оно очевидно.

Introduction — оба предложения задания в одном через while

The [chart 1] shows [what], while the [chart 2] illustrates [what] [in / during TIME].

The pie graph displays the principal causes why farming land deteriorates while the table illustrates how three regions of the planet were affected by these factors in the 1990s.

Overview — по предложению на каждый график

We can clearly see from the [chart 1] that [two main features]. The [chart 2] shows that [two main features].

Body 1 — только первый график

The [chart 1] represents [number] [categories], with [A] representing the biggest proportion at [just over a third]. [B] and [C] constitute [nearly one third] and [just over a quarter] respectively.

Body 2 — только второй график (+ сравнение, если есть)

The [chart 2] shows that [detail of feature 1]. [Detail of feature 2], and [C] was marginally higher with a value of [number].

Process diagram: производство лапши

Источник: пост 34 (там же схема задания). Задание: «The diagram below shows how instant noodles are manufactured».

Процесс выполняют люди и машины — это linear man-made process, поэтому почти все стадии описываем в Passive Voice.

1. Introduction

Только перефразируем задание. Без стадий, подробностей и своего мнения.

The diagram outlines the stages involved in the production of [product].

The diagram outlines the stages involved in the production of instant noodles.

2. Overview

Процесс целиком: тип процесса, число основных стадий, первая и последняя стадии, главная трансформация.

Overall, the production of [product] is a linear, man-made process comprising [number] main stages, beginning with [first stage] and ending with [final stage]. Throughout the process, [initial material] is transformed into [final product] through [main transformations].

Overall, the production of instant noodles is a linear, man-made process comprising eight main stages, beginning with the storage of flour and ending with the labelling and sealing of the finished cups. Throughout the process, flour is transformed into a packaged food product through mixing, shaping, cooking, drying and packaging.

3. Как делить Body

Не «4 стадии + 4 стадии», а по логике. Body 1 (стадии 1–4): мука превращается в полоски теста. Body 2 (стадии 5–8): полоски превращаются в готовый упакованный продукт.

4. Body 1

Initially, [raw material] is [first action]. It is then [second action], where [additional materials] are added. The resulting [material] is subsequently [third action] to form [intermediate product], which is then [fourth action].

Initially, flour is delivered by truck and stored in large silos. It is then transferred to a mixer, where water and oil are added. The resulting dough is passed through a series of rollers to form thin sheets, which are subsequently cut into narrow strips.

5. Body 2

Next, [intermediate product] is [next action] before being [following action]. Once this stage has been completed, [product] is placed into [container], with [additional ingredients] added. Finally, [final action], producing [finished product].

Next, the strips are shaped into circular noodle discs before being cooked in oil and dried. The dried discs are then placed into individual cups, to which vegetables and spices are added. Finally, the cups are labelled and sealed, producing ready-to-sell cups of instant noodles.

Естественное развитие: эволюция лошади

Источник: посты 35 (схема), 36 и 37. Задание: «The diagrams below show the development of the horse over a period of 40 million years».

WHAT → BIG PICTURE → EARLY CHANGES → FINAL RESULT

Это natural linear development. Следим сразу за двумя линиями изменений: внешний вид и размер лошади; строение стопы.

Introduction

Три вопроса: что развивалось? за какой период? какой части уделено особое внимание? Конкретные изменения пока не описываем.

The diagrams illustrate how [subject] evolved over a period of [time], with particular emphasis on changes in [specific feature].

The diagrams illustrate how the horse evolved over a period of 40 million years, with particular emphasis on changes in the structure of its foot.

Overview

Вся история развития, но без названий видов и дат. Две главные тенденции: лошадь становилась крупнее и выше; стопа упрощалась — четыре пальца превратились в одно копыто.

Overall, [subject] became progressively [main physical change], while [specific feature] became considerably [second change]. In particular, [initial structure] was gradually reduced/transformed into [final structure].

Overall, the horse became progressively larger and taller, while its legs lengthened and its foot structure became considerably simpler. In particular, the original four toes were gradually reduced to a single large hoof.

Как делить Body

Body 1 — ранние стадии (Eohippus → Mesohippus): начало изменений. Body 2 — поздние стадии (Merychippus → modern horse): дальнейшее развитие и конечный результат.

Body 1

[Time] ago, [first stage] was [description], while its [specific feature] consisted of [initial structure]. By [later time], [second stage] had become [physical change]. At the same time, [specific feature] had changed from [initial form] to [new form].

Forty million years ago, Eohippus was a relatively small animal with a curved back, short legs and a short tail. Its foot consisted of four slender toes. Ten million years later, Mesohippus had grown noticeably larger and developed longer legs. At the same time, the number of toes had fallen to three, with the middle toe becoming slightly more prominent than the two outer ones.

Body 2

By [time], [third stage] had become [next physical change] and more closely resembled [final form]. Although its [specific feature] still had [structure], [important element] had become [change]. In the modern [subject], [final physical state], while [final structural change].

By 15 million years ago, Merychippus had increased further in size and more closely resembled the modern horse, particularly because of its longer legs and straighter back. Although its foot still had three toes, the central one had become substantially larger. In the modern horse, the animal has reached its greatest size, while the side toes have disappeared completely, leaving only one well-developed hoof.

Карты: Grange Park, 1920 и сегодня

Источник: посты 47 (карта) и 49. Готовый ответ — 155 слов.

TIME → CHANGES → INTRODUCTION → OVERVIEW → BODY 1 → BODY 2

Сначала определяем время, затем сортируем изменения и только после этого начинаем писать.

Шаг 1. Время

Past Simple — старая картаIn 1920, the park contained several rose gardens. Present Perfect Passive — изменение к настоящемуThe stage has been replaced by an amphitheatre. Present Simple — как сейчасThe entrances remain in the same positions.

Шаг 2. Сортируем изменения по четырём категориям

remained unchangedсеверо-западный розарий; входы с Arnold Avenue и Eldon Street was replaced bystage → amphitheatre; fountain → central rose garden; NE rose garden → café; pond → children’s play area; glasshouse → water feature was removedsouth-western rose garden; прежние лавочки по краям was addedunderground car park; отдельный въезд; лавочки вокруг центрального розария

Шаг 3. Introduction

Что сравнивается и какие периоды показаны. Изменения пока не описываем.

The maps compare the layout of [place] in [past year] with its present-day design.

The maps compare the layout of Grange Park in 1920 with its present-day design.

Шаг 4. Overview

Не перечисляем объекты: главная трансформация + что сохранилось.

Overall, [place] has been extensively modernised, with most of its [old features] replaced by [new type of facilities]. However, [unchanged features] have remained unchanged.

Overall, the park has been extensively modernised, with most of its gardens and older structures replaced by recreational facilities. However, the north-western rose garden and the two main entrances have remained unchanged.

Шаг 5. Body 1

Главная ошибка — Body 1 про старую карту, Body 2 про новую: получаются два списка, а не сравнение. Делим по территории: Body 1 — запад и центр, Body 2 — восток и входы.

In [year], the [area] contained [old features]. [One feature] has since been replaced by [new feature], while [another feature] has been removed. In the centre, [old feature] has given way to [new feature].

In 1920, the western side contained a stage for musicians, a south-western rose garden and several seats. The stage has since been replaced by an amphitheatre, while the south-western garden has been removed. In the centre, the fountain has given way to a square rose garden surrounded by seating, although the north-western garden has been retained.

Шаг 6. Body 2

Три старых объекта → три новых одним предложением через respectively. Порядок объектов обязан совпадать. Затем новый объект и сохранившиеся входы.

On the [area] side, the former [A], [B] and [C] have been replaced by [X], [Y] and [Z], respectively.

On the eastern side, the former rose garden, pond and glasshouse have been replaced by a café, children’s play area and water feature, respectively. An underground car park with a separate entrance has also been added in the south-east. Meanwhile, the entrances on Arnold Avenue and Eldon Street remain in their original positions.

Два процесса сразу: Venus flytrap

Источник: пост 140 (там же схема). Задание: «The diagram shows how small and large Venus Flytrap species handle small prey to optimize their use of digestive enzymes».

Overview

Не перечислять все шесть картинок, а объединить их в два процесса и сразу назвать главное различие.

Small trap: captures prey → digestion → nutrient absorption
Large trap: closes → prey escapes → trap reopens

Body

Body 1 — первый механизм от начала до конца одной последовательностью. Body 2 начинаем со сходства («The second sequence shows a similar initial stage in a larger trap»), затем через However и Unlike переходим к главному различию.

Причинно-следственная связь

Описываем не только стадии, но и почему одна вытекает из другой.

Слабо: The insect touches the hairs. The trap closes.

Сильно: This contact activates the mechanism of the trap, causing its two sides to snap shut rapidly.

Полный образец ответа из поста — в тренажёре (задание «Venus flytrap»).

Чек-лист перед сдачей Task 1

сводка по разборам
  • Introduction только перефразирует задание.
  • Overview начинается с Overall и показывает картину целиком — без дат, названий стадий и мелких деталей.
  • Body разделены по логике (этап трансформации или территория), а не механически пополам.
  • Man-made процесс — Passive Voice; карты — Past Simple, Present Perfect Passive, Present Simple.
  • Между стадиями есть связки и причина-следствие, а не список коротких предложений.
  • Нет собственного мнения. Не меньше 150 слов.

Band 8.0 Opinion Essay

Erdley

Источник: пост 17. Видеоразбор целого эссе «без сложной лексики» — пост 21 (10:34, смотреть в Telegram).

Some people believe that exams are an inappropriate way of measuring students’ performance and should be replaced by continuous assessment. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Many schools and universities use final examinations to measure students’ academic performance. Some people believe that this method is ineffective and should be replaced with continuous assessment. I completely agree because regular assessment shows students’ abilities over time and allows a wider range of skills to be evaluated.

The first reason is that a single exam cannot always provide a fair picture of a student’s knowledge. It only shows how well someone performs on one particular day. Even a capable student may receive a low mark because they are nervous, unwell or unable to understand one question correctly. Continuous assessment, in contrast, is based on several assignments completed throughout the course. For example, teachers can consider students’ homework, written tasks and class performance when calculating their final grade. As a result, the grade is less likely to be affected by one unsuccessful exam.

Another reason is that continuous assessment can measure skills that traditional examinations often ignore. Projects, presentations and reports require students to research information, solve problems and communicate their ideas clearly. These abilities are important both at university and in future employment. Regular assessment also encourages students to study consistently throughout the course. By contrast, examinations may lead learners to memorise large amounts of information shortly before the test and forget it soon afterwards. Some people may argue that continuous assessment is less objective because different teachers can give different marks. However, this problem can be reduced by using clear marking criteria and having important assignments checked by more than one teacher.

In conclusion, I fully agree that examinations should be replaced with continuous assessment because it provides a more reliable picture of students’ knowledge and evaluates a broader range of useful skills.

IELTS Advantage

1. Opinion — agree or disagree

Структура — Essay Structures, урок. Шаблон для заполнения собран мной из опорных фраз их образцов.

Как узнать

Do you agree or disagree? · To what extent do you agree or disagree? · What is your opinion? Сюда же: Is this a positive or negative development?

Структура по предложениям

Introductionперефраз вопроса → мнение + две главные идеиBody 1topic sentence → объяснение → примерBody 2topic sentence → объяснение → примерConclusionитог: главные идеи и мнение

Introduction

It is argued that [paraphrase of the statement]. This essay [agrees / disagrees] with this [completely] because [reason 1] and [reason 2].

Body 1

[Topic sentence: reason 1]. [Explain: this means that … / as a result, …]. For example, [specific example].

Body 2

[Topic sentence: reason 2] also [ … ]. [Explain]. For instance, [specific example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, [restate your position] because [reason 1 in other words], and [reason 2 in other words].

Важно

Позиция одна и та же в каждом абзаце. Эссе Виолетты выше — тот же тип с добавленным контраргументом; на экзамене выберите один вариант и не смешивайте. «I completely agree» и «This essay agrees» оба допустимы.

2. Discussion — discuss both views

Структура — Essay Structures, урок. Шаблон для заполнения собран мной из опорных фраз их образцов.

Some argue that [view A], while others believe that [view B]. This essay agrees that [your side].

Body 1

On the one hand, [view A]. [Why people think so]. It is therefore agreed that [ … ] / However, this essay disagrees with this because [ … ]. [Example] is a prime example.

Body 2

However, many disagree and feel that [view B]. [Why people think so]. Despite this, [your reason to agree or disagree]. For instance, [example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, while [view you reject, fairly stated], [view you support]. [One sentence: why it is more important].

Важно

Мнение просят почти всегда — оно должно быть во введении и в заключении. Даже если вам близки обе стороны, выберите одну: так ответ яснее.

Как узнать

Discuss the advantages and disadvantages. · What are the advantages and disadvantages?

Структура по предложениям

Introduction

It is argued that [paraphrase]. [A1] and [A2] are the main advantages, whereas [D1] and [D2] are the main disadvantages.

Body 1

The main advantages of [topic] are [A1] and [A2]. [Explain A1]. Furthermore, [explain A2]. A prime example of this is [example].

Body 2

The main disadvantages associated with [topic] are [D1] and [D2]. [Explain D1]. As well as this, [explain D2]. For instance, [example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, the benefits of [topic], such as [A1] and [A2], must be weighed against the drawbacks, such as [D1] and [D2].

Важно

Мнение здесь не спрашивают — не пишите, какая сторона лучше.

4. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Структура — Essay Structures, урок. Шаблон для заполнения собран мной из опорных фраз их образцов.

Как узнать

Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages? · Do the benefits outweigh the drawbacks?

Структура по предложениям

Introductionперефраз → thesis: что перевешивает → outlineBody 1 — сильная сторонадовод 1 → почему он сильный → пример; довод 2 → почему сильный → примерBody 2 — слабая сторонадовод → почему он не такой весомый → примерConclusionитог и позиция ещё раз

Introduction

[Paraphrase of the statement]. This essay will argue that the advantages of this outweigh the drawbacks. The essay will first demonstrate that [A1] and [A2], followed by an analysis of how the primary disadvantage, namely [D], is not valid.

Body 1

The main reason to [do X] is that [A1]. That is to say that [explain]. For example, [example]. In addition, [A2]. [Explain]. For instance, [example].

Body 2

Those who oppose this say that [D]. However, [why this is not a strong argument]. For example, [example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, [A1] and [A2] mean that the advantages of [topic] far outweigh the disadvantage of [D].

Важно

Вопрос не о количестве плюсов и минусов, а об их весе. Сторона, которую вы выбрали, получает большой абзац, вторая — короткий, с объяснением, почему она слабее.

5. Problem and solution · cause and solution

Структура — Essay Structures, урок. Шаблон для заполнения собран мной из опорных фраз их образцов.

Как узнать

What problems does this cause and what are the possible solutions? · What are the causes of this and how can it be solved?

Структура по предложениям

Introductionперефраз → outline: проблемы и решенияBody 1назвать проблемы → объяснить первую → объяснить вторую → примерBody 2назвать решения → объяснить первое → объяснить второе → примерConclusionитог

Introduction

[Paraphrase of the situation]. This essay will first suggest that the biggest problems caused by this are [P1] and [P2] and then argue that [S1] and [S2] are the most viable solutions.

Body 1

The principal problems with [topic] are [P1] and [P2]. [Explain P1]. As well as this, [explain P2]. For example, [example].

Body 2

Solutions to these problems are [S1] and [S2]. If [S1 happens], this will [result]. Also, [S2 and its result]. For instance, [example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, the main problems with [topic] are [P1] and [P2]. These can be solved through [S1] and [S2].

Важно

Пишите только о том, что спросили. Если спрашивают causes — в Body 1 «The main causes of [issue] are [C1] and [C2]», о последствиях ни слова. Каждое решение должно отвечать на свою проблему.

6. Two-part question

Структура — Essay Structures. Шаблон для заполнения собран мной из опорных фраз их образцов.

Как узнать

Утверждение и два отдельных вопроса: Why is this? What can be done? · What factors…? How realistic…?

Структура по предложениям

Introductionперефраз → outline с ответами на оба вопросаBody 1прямой ответ на первый вопрос → почему → подробнее → примерBody 2прямой ответ на второй вопрос → почему → подробнее → примерConclusionитог

Introduction

[Paraphrase of the statement]. This essay will first suggest [answer to question 1], and it will then state that [answer to question 2].

Body 1

[Direct answer to question 1]. [Explain why]. [Explain further]. For example, [example].

Body 2

With regard to the question of [question 2], [direct answer]. [Explain why]. [Explain further]. For example, [example].

Conclusion

In conclusion, [answer 1 in other words]; however, [answer 2 in other words].

Важно

Один вопрос — один абзац. Самая частая ошибка — ответить только на первый.
